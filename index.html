My Photos Accessor lets you view your entire Google Photos library in a clean, no‑clutter gallery.

📌 Purpose of this app:
This application is a lightweight viewer for your Google Photos. It does not store, share, or analyse your images – it simply displays them using Google’s official API. You remain in full control of your data at all times.
🔐 Connect with Google

About My Photos Accessor

This app was built for people who want a fast, straightforward way to browse their Google Photos without extra features or distractions. It uses Google’s OAuth 2.0 to get read‑only access to your library – nothing more.

  • Read‑only access: we only retrieve your photos to show them to you.
  • No data retention: we never store your images, albums, or personal information.
  • Transparent: you can revoke access at any time from your Google Account.
