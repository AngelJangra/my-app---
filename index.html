About this app

This app was built with one clear purpose: to show you all your Google Photos in one convenient place. It uses Google’s official API to fetch your library and display it in a responsive, easy‑to‑browse grid.

  • Read‑only access – we only view your photos, we never modify or delete them.
  • No data storage – we don’t save any of your images or personal information.
  • Full control – you can revoke access anytime from your Google Account.
