Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Memories (we, us, or our) is a simple tool that lets you view your Google Photos library. We take your privacy seriously.

What we collect

We only access the bare minimum needed to show you your photos:

  • Google Account access – with your permission, we read your photo library via Google's official API. We never store your photos, albums, or any metadata on our servers.
  • Session tokens – we temporarily keep an OAuth token in your browser session so you stay signed in. It's never saved to disk.

How we use it

Your data is used only to display your photos to you. We do not:

  • Share your data with anyone.
  • Use it for advertising or analytics.
  • Store any of your images or personal info.

Third‑party services

We use Google's OAuth 2.0 and Photos Library API. Your interaction with those is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Your control

  • You can revoke our access anytime at Google Account Permissions.
  • We don't retain any data – so there's nothing to delete on our side.

Contact

Questions? Drop us a line at khiladi@duck.com.
