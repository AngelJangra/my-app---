Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

PhotoAccess (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information required to provide our service:

  • Google Account Data – With your explicit consent, we access your Google Photos library to display your photos. We never store your photos or any metadata on our servers.
  • Authentication Tokens – We temporarily store OAuth access tokens in your browser session to maintain your login state. These tokens are never saved to disk or shared.
  • Usage Data – We do not collect analytics, trackers, or any personally identifiable information beyond what is necessary for the service to function.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we access is used solely for the purpose of:

  • Displaying your Google Photos in a convenient gallery interface.
  • Allowing you to view, search, and share photos (if you choose to do so).
  • Authenticating your identity to Google via OAuth 2.0.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any other commercial purpose.

3. Data Storage and Security

All photo data is fetched directly from Google’s servers and is never stored on our servers. We do not retain copies of your photos, albums, or metadata. Your OAuth tokens are stored exclusively in your browser’s session storage and are cleared when you close your browser. We implement industry‑standard security measures to protect your data during transmission.

4. Third‑Party Services

We use Google’s OAuth 2.0 and Photos Library API to access your photos. Your interaction with those services is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not use any other third‑party services that collect your data.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Revoke Access – You can revoke our app’s access to your Google Photos at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions page.
  • Data Deletion – Since we store no data, there is nothing to delete on our side. All your information remains solely within your Google Account.
  • Contact – If you have any questions, you can reach us at support@photoaccess.app.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@photoaccess.app.
